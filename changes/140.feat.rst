`SlaterKoster` for two-center tight-binding models

`SlaterKoster` gives the Hamiltonian and overlap of a Slater-Koster model with
s, p and d shells, through ``H.construct(sk)``. An integral is a constant,
a step function ``(R_list, values)`` or a function of the distance.
Values can depend on the spin: ``[up, down]`` or a 2x2 spin matrix.

`SlaterKoster.from_hamiltonian` extracts the two-center integrals from a
Hamiltonian, for example of Siesta, as in McMahan and Klepeis (1997).
The integrals of the bonds of each length are a least-squares fit.
``H - H2`` with ``H2.construct(sk)`` is the part that is not two-center.

`SlaterKoster.merge` joins models, for example one for each species pair.
A coupling that two models have raises an error.

`sisl.io.dftb.skfSileDFTB` reads DFTB Slater-Koster files (``A-B.skf``) into a
`SlaterKoster` with the interpolation of DFTB+.
