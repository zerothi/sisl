Refactored the electrode self-energies and Green function of ``sisl_toolbox.btd``

``PivotSelfEnergy`` and ``DownfoldSelfEnergy`` are replaced by ``Electrode``
(abstract base class), ``BlochElectrode`` and ``DownfoldElectrode``.
``DownfoldElectrode`` takes any electrode (e.g. another downfolded electrode),
the Hamiltonian and the downfolding orbitals/blocks explicitly, and has no
knowledge of the device region. ``RealSpaceElectrode`` handles electrodes with
multiple semi-infinite directions, from a ``RealSpaceSE``/``RealSpaceSI``
or a GF file.
``DeviceGreen`` takes the electrodes as ``(electrode, orbitals)`` pairs and
handles all permutations into the device region. Its ``permute`` argument is a
``PermuteBTD``, a permutation table, or a ``btd_partition`` method; a TBtrans
output file is no longer accepted.
``DeviceGreen.from_tbt`` creates the Green function from the regions and
permutation tables of a TBtrans output file and the electrode Hamiltonians.
``DeviceGreen.from_fdf`` now only reads the fdf file (and the Hamiltonian
files it refers to, relative to the fdf file), and calculates the downfolding
regions and permutation tables. It no longer accepts ``use_tbt_se``.
Electrodes can be read from fdf files with ``Electrode.from_fdf``, which
returns the electrode class matching the electrode block.
``TBT.Atoms.Device`` defaults to all atoms not in the electrodes nor in the
buffer, and may contain electrode atoms: electrodes fully in the device region
are not downfolded, electrodes partially in the device region are partially
downfolded (only their atoms outside the device region). ``DownfoldElectrode``
and ``downfold_partition`` accept electrodes partially in the last block (device
region), the electrode self-energy on those orbitals is added to the downfolded
self-energy.
``DeviceGreen`` electrodes may be given as ``(electrode, orbitals, bulk)``,
where ``bulk`` uses the electrode Hamiltonian in the device region.
``btd_partition`` accepts ``dense`` blocks (e.g. electrode orbitals), which
are guaranteed to span at most 2 neighboring blocks.
``DeviceGreen.transmission`` and ``DeviceGreen.scattering_matrix`` accept
``**kwargs`` (e.g. ``spin``).
``Electrode.from_fdf`` and ``DeviceGreen.from_fdf`` accept ``use_gf`` (default
false) to read the self-energies from the ``GF`` files of the electrode blocks,
which is required for real-space electrodes (e.g. ``semi-inf-direction ab``).
Without chemical potentials and bias, TBtrans electrodes are all at 0 (as TBtrans).
``Electrode.from_fdf`` returns a ``DownfoldElectrode`` for electrodes not in the
device region (``downfold=False`` returns the electrode itself).
``downfold_partition`` finds the downfolding regions (and their BTD blocks) of
electrodes. ``btd_partition`` and ``downfold_partition`` are agnostic to atoms
and orbitals, they partition the rows of the passed matrix (``H.toSparseAtom()``
for atoms).
``DeviceGreen`` electrode indices and ``permute`` (arrays or ``PermuteBTD``)
are atoms, or ``{"atoms": idx}`` / ``{"orbitals": idx}``; atomic partitions
are expanded to orbitals. ``DownfoldElectrode(electrode, H, elec, down)`` uses
the same index convention, ``down`` is a ``PermuteBTD`` (explicit blocks) or
the indices of the downfolding region (order and blocks found automatically). Method strings accept the TBtrans ``atom+``/``orb+``
prefixes and default to atoms, as do ``TBT.BTD.Pivot.Device`` and
``TBT.BTD.Pivot.Elec[s]`` in ``from_fdf``. ``Electrode.orbs`` holds the device orbitals of an
electrode's self-energy, and ``DeviceGreen`` accepts such electrodes without
explicit indices.

Fixed spin (non-colinear) expansion of downfolded self-energies, caching of
downfolded self-energies and ``DeviceGreen`` quantities with different
``spin``/``dtype`` arguments, and the spin expansion of ``DeviceGreen`` when
the Hamiltonian is larger than the device region. Eigenchannels now use the
``spin`` of the scattering states. The ``delta-Ef`` and ``V-fraction``
electrode block options were never read from the fdf file, and a ``bulk false``
electrode block option was read as true. Self-energies from GF files were
returned as the bulk self-energy (:math:`E\mathbf S - \mathbf H - \boldsymbol\Sigma`)
instead of :math:`\boldsymbol\Sigma`.
The electrode ``name`` is an optional keyword-only argument, e.g.
``BlochElectrode(se, bloch, name="Left")``. Unnamed electrodes are referenced
in ``DeviceGreen`` by their index (or the electrode object), names are matched
case-insensitively and must be unique. ``DeviceGreen(..., permute="electrode=<name|index>")``
starts the BTD partitioning from that electrode (defaults to the first).
