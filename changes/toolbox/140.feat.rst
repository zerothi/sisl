Slater-Koster reference Hamiltonians

``sisl_toolbox.models.slater_koster.H.ref`` has the :math:`sp^3s^*` model of
Vogl, Hjalmarson and Dow (``Vogl1983``, 16 semiconductors) and the carbon model
of Xu, Wang, Chan and Ho (``Xu1992``). A ``geometry`` argument gets the orbitals
of the model by atomic number.
