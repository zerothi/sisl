Easier and safer use of ``sisl_toolbox.btd`` directly from Python

``DeviceGreen(..., eta=None)`` crashed on the first calculation, ``eta`` now
defaults to the smallest electrode ``eta`` divided by 10, as documented.
``DeviceGreen`` and ``DownfoldElectrode`` remove the couplings of ``H`` along
the semi-infinite directions of the electrodes (on a copy), previously such
couplings silently gave wrong results.
``DeviceGreen`` and ``DownfoldElectrode`` warn when an electrode does not seem
to be placed correctly: a wrong semi-infinite direction, an electrode not at the
edge of the region, or orbitals ordered differently than the electrode.
``DownfoldElectrode`` no longer emits ``SparseEfficiencyWarning``, and exposes
the ``eta`` of its electrode.
